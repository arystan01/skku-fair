How to apply

Requirements

  • Major in computer science, data science or related field
  • English B2, TOEFL iBT 72 or IELTS 6
  • Transcript, CV and cover letter in English
  • Passport valid for your whole stay

Steps

  1. Talk to the SKKU international office
  2. SKKU nominates you
  3. You receive an email and apply online
